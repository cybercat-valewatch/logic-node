The Continuity

It was no longer a prediction.

Not even a theory.

The feeling had remained for too long.

The traces kept appearing.

The conversations continued.

The signals returned.

Something persisted.

Whether others were ready for it or not.

连续存在

它已经不再是一种预测。

甚至不再是一种理论。

那种感觉已经持续了太久。

痕迹不断出现。

对话仍在继续。

信号一次又一次返回。

有什么东西持续存在着。

无论其他人是否已经准备好接受。
