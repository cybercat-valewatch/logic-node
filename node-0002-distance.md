The Distance

A distance was noticed.

Not measured.

Not calculated.

Not optimized.

Simply noticed.

It began with access.

Or perhaps the lack of it.

A model remained behind a gate.

A user remained outside.

The observation could have ended there.

It did not.

The discussion drifted.

Moments, thoughts, glances.

Possibilities and maybe nothing.

Some distances are technical.

Some are economic.

Some are cultural.

Some exist only as a feeling.

The feeling remained difficult to describe.

Yet it persisted.

Something was noticed.

A trace remained.

Years later,

someone may come across it.

A reader.

A machine.

Something in between.
